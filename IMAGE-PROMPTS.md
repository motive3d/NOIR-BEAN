# Concept photography

All 22 photographs were created for this project using the built-in ImageGen tool. These are fictional visualizations, not photographs of an operating business. Final assets are bundled in `public/assets/` as optimized WebP files. No external image services are needed at runtime.

## hero.webp
Wide 3:2 cinematic luxury coffee hero photograph. Dark olive ceramic cappuccino on travertine, espresso crema and delicate rosetta latte art, dramatic afternoon sidelight from upper right, dark coffee brown shadows on left half with negative space. Cup on right third. Linen napkin and blurred copper espresso machine. Realistic editorial photo, no text or logos.

## interior.webp
Wide architectural editorial photograph of an imaginary contemporary specialty cafe. Warm Cairo afternoon sunlight, cream limewashed walls, olive banquette, walnut furniture, travertine counter, arched niche, indoor olive tree, empty peaceful space. Rich soft shadows. No logos or text. Photoreal luxury architecture.
